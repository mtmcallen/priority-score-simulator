Multi-Photo Answer Option — Mobile Prototype

Interactive iOS & Android mockup based on Rounding Assistant. Check an answer, tap the camera icon, choose up to 10 existing photos, then see thumbnails listed under the answer with × to remove.

Flow

  1. 1Check an answer (e.g. Bathroom not clean)
  2. 2Tap camera icon → action sheet
  3. 3Choose existing → multi-select picker
  4. 4Tap Choose (top right) — up to 10 photos
  5. 5Thumbnails appear under answer; tap × to remove

Toggle iOS / Android on the right. Picker enforces 10 photos max per answer and per selection batch.
