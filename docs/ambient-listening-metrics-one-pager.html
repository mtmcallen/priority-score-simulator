Why opportunity areas matter most

  • Prisma: 72% of AL rounds surfaced a patient concern vs 34% without — more than double
  • Hartford: 59% vs 18% — over 3× the concerns identified
  • UMass: 43% vs 36%, a steadier lift on an already-high baseline
  • These are concerns patients raised that would otherwise go unrecorded — not a decline in experience

Adoption signals

  • 417 AL rounds across 57 rounders at three health systems
  • Prisma is expanding system-wide and has the widest rounder adoption (32)
  • Consent declines are rare on AL rounds — 15 total across all three sites
  • Rounders trust the drafts — 0% override at UMass and Prisma, 1.7% at Hartford

What this means for rounders

Ambient Listening gives UMass rounders back about 12 seconds of typing per round on a fair median clock. On Looker’s start-to-submit averages for May–July, Prisma and Hartford AL rounds take about 5 and 4 minutes vs 18 and 30 minutes without AL on the same scripts. The real change is still what ends up in the record: more of the script completed, 5–20× more patient quotes captured, and far more patient concerns surfaced for leaders to act on — with every AL-generated issue closed out at all three sites.

Methodology & caveats: Rounding metrics from Looker (patient_rounds_activity), May 5 – Sep 9, 2026, where AL means the round produced AI-generated answers. Non-AL is limited to the same rounding scripts each site uses AL on, but not to the same rounders or patient mix. AL samples are small (58–187 rounds per site) against large non-AL controls, so early-adopter selection bias is likely. Higher opportunity-area and issue rates indicate better capture of patient concerns, not worsening experience. Care gram counts may include multiple recipients per care gram. UMass documentation time is median interaction duration from the Patient Interactions export (Feb 25 – Aug 24, 2026; completed rounds; n=63 AL vs 304 non-AL; durations >30 min excluded). Prisma and Hartford documentation time is Looker average minutes from round start to submit, May 5 – Jul 31, 2026, interactive rounds on the same AL scripts (Prisma n=151 vs 100,310; Hartford n=130 vs 17,663). Non-AL medians on that clock are 0 minutes — averages are pulled up by sessions left open — so those % reductions are not the same measurement as the UMass median. AL medians on that clock are 4 min (Prisma) and 1 min (Hartford).
