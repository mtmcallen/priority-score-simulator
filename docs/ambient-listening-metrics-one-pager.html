What this means for nurses

When you use Ambient Listening, you spend slightly less time documenting and capture dramatically more of what patients tell you. Instead of tapping through checkboxes, the AI drafts answers from your conversation — including the stories, concerns, and improvement ideas that usually get lost. You still review and submit every answer. The difference is leaders get 4× more improvement narratives and 18× more detail per round to act on.

Methodology & caveats: Analysis includes completed patient rounds only (Ability to Round = Yes). AL sample is small (n=63) vs non-AL (n=304), creating potential selection bias — early adopters may round differently. Checkbox completion rates are similar between groups; the primary AL benefit is qualitative capture (free-text details), not more checkboxes filled. Duration outliers >30 min excluded (6 rounds). Per-rounder duration varies widely; aggregate median is the most reliable comparison. Care gram rates are similar between groups given small AL sample.
